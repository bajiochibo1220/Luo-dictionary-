# Using Luo Heritage

This guide describes the PRD implementation. Collections can be empty until approved material is published. No class membership is required to explore the public library.

## Discover and download

Choose a community, search a collection and use the bottom media controls to show all content, images, audio, video or transcripts. Switch between the community language and its English version. English remains a translation of that community's material. Open an item for its full text. Use its download links for text and eligible attached files. Restricted material requires assigned access; a link does not override consent.

Use Knowledge connections to explore relationships explicitly supplied by editors. Places shows only locations separately approved for public mapping; coordinates are approximate. Load the map when needed or download the approved locations as GeoJSON. Sacred or privately recorded GPS coordinates are not automatically mapped.

Folktales and heritage items can offer a guided illustrated gallery when approved images and text exist. Artifact items with an OBJ model offer an interactive viewer: load it, drag or use arrow keys to rotate, zoom and reset. Other model formats remain downloadable; this viewer supports bounded OBJ geometry without external textures.

## Learn voluntarily

Learning resources cover beginner through advanced levels and reading, writing, listening and speaking. Save progress after activities. Games can offer vocabulary races, word puzzles, memory pairs, proverb matching, story completion, treasure clues and artifact identification when editors publish suitable questions and images.

Language battles require signing in. Open a published game, create a room and share its invitation code with another signed-in person. The host starts after at least two people join. Answers are checked on the server; participants use pseudonyms. Scores appear when the round ends. An expired room or changed source requires a new round. Internet access is required for multiplayer.

## Tutor and media assistance

Sign in, choose a community and select conversation, translation, grammar, writing, story practice or vocabulary. Read and select the processing consent before submitting. AI suggestions can be wrong. Generated stories are practice fiction, not verified cultural records. Vocabulary practice requires at least four distinct published dictionary translations and uses server grading.

Speech controls depend on your browser and installed voices. A missing community voice is reported instead of substituting a different language. Browser dictation may use your browser provider's service. Personal recordings stop after one minute. They remain on your device unless you separately choose AI transcription; you can preview and download them. There is no calibrated pronunciation score.

AI media tools offer OCR, transcription, image description and story illustration using eligible published sources. Choose a source, give processing consent and run the tool. Download the resulting draft. To add it to the repository, use the normal contribution and editorial process. An AI-generated illustration must be labelled as an illustration, never evidence of a historical appearance.

## Research and settings

Open **Help & training** in the navigation menu for your account's manual. Public accounts see the public library guide; assigned staff, teaching and research accounts see their own guides. Search the topics or download the current guide. An account with several assigned duties can switch between those guides.

Public research publications, reports and datasets can be explored without privileged access. Assigned researchers and teachers see additional material only where its consent and restrictions permit. Private exports require research or curation permissions and must stay in approved storage.

Open the account menu for Settings & profile, saved conversations and sign out. The menu closes using its trigger, outside click or Escape. Interface language is separate from your assigned roles. Missing reviewed translations fall back to English; report missing wording to an editor through the project's support process.

Offline saved content and compatible learning progress are available through the existing offline library. AI, new downloads, research exports, maps and multiplayer need a connection. Synchronize queued progress after reconnecting.
