# Using Luo Heritage

Updated 28 October 2026.

## More room for collection content

On wide screens, collection search and the content-language selector appear in the top masthead. On smaller screens they sit directly above the collection title. The compact bottom bar filters images, audio, videos and transcripts. Descriptions open in full; use **Read less** to collapse them. Images and videos fit their available space without cropping, and narrow content areas stack media above descriptions.

## Language and navigation

The public interface starts in English. An editor can save approved Dholuo wording in **Edit system text**. Each saved label then shows Dholuo first and smaller English underneath; untranslated labels stay English. This includes login, sign out, navigation, settings and the in-app manual. Use the community selector to choose a community. The separate content-language control changes a record's displayed language without changing menus. Administrative workspaces always stay in English.

## A simpler cultural assistant

Open **AI cultural assistant**, enter a question, select **Allow AI processing for this chat**, and send. Suggested questions fill the input so you can edit them first. **Chat settings** contains answer-language and processing information. Expand **Sources** and **Related media** when needed. **Your chats** opens saved conversations after sign-in; **New chat** starts another conversation. Use **X** to close the assistant. Escape closes an open menu or history panel first, then closes the assistant. AI answers can be mistaken; check source material.

Some public items are marked **Review in progress**. Authorized staff may publish an uploaded snapshot while review continues. Unfinished edits stay private; publishing the reviewed version automatically replaces the earlier public package at the same link.

This guide describes the PRD implementation. Collections can be empty until approved material is published. No class membership is required to explore the public library.

The dashboard has shortcuts for listening, learning, the tutor and **My manual**. Teachers and researchers also see entry cards relevant to their assigned duties. Navigation icons accompany labels; use the menu button on phones to open the sidebar. The same interface is shared with packaged mobile clients.

The registration form reports unavailable community languages and provides a retry action. Select an active community, enter the required details and accept the Terms and Privacy Policy. Password visibility buttons can show or hide the password. A successful account registration continues to sign-in; failures retain a clear retry or login path. Your assigned roles determine the dashboard reached after login.

## Discover and download

Open **Start here** for paths for children, youth, adults, schools, universities, researchers, cultural institutions, museums, tourists, diaspora communities, linguists, AI researchers and government cultural agencies. Choosing a path only changes recommendations; it does not change your account or grant access. Children can explore stories and riddles with a trusted adult without supplying a child's personal details.

The same page lists all fifteen modules and all twenty-seven PRD subjects. Select a subject to browse its published, publicly permitted records across active collections. Search within that subject or load more results. The dictionary subject also includes the structured dictionary. Other subjects depend on editors classifying records using **Subject area**; empty results do not mean records have been invented or automatically approved.

The sidebar keeps primary destinations visible. Expand **Collections** for individual archives, or **More & settings** for places, cultural connections, AI media tools, courses, contributions, settings and help. The current collection opens automatically. The public research portal is always reachable from primary navigation; restricted research still requires an assigned role.

Choose a community, search a collection and use the bottom media controls to show all content, images, audio, video or transcripts. Switch between the community language and its English version. English remains a translation of that community's material. Open an item for its full text. Use its download links for text and eligible attached files. Restricted material requires assigned access; a link does not override consent.

Use Knowledge connections to explore relationships explicitly supplied by editors. Places shows only locations separately approved for public mapping; coordinates are approximate. Load the map when needed or download the approved locations as GeoJSON. Sacred or privately recorded GPS coordinates are not automatically mapped.

Folktales and heritage items can offer a guided illustrated gallery when approved images and text exist. Artifact items with an OBJ model offer an interactive viewer: load it, drag or use arrow keys to rotate, zoom and reset. Other model formats remain downloadable; this viewer supports bounded OBJ geometry without external textures.

## Learn voluntarily

Learning resources cover beginner through advanced levels and reading, writing, listening and speaking. Save progress after activities. Games can offer vocabulary races, word puzzles, memory pairs, proverb matching, story completion, treasure clues and artifact identification when editors publish suitable questions and images.

Language battles require signing in. Open a published game, create a room and share its invitation code with another signed-in person. The host starts after at least two people join. Answers are checked on the server; participants use pseudonyms. Scores appear when the round ends. An expired room or changed source requires a new round. Internet access is required for multiplayer.

## Tutor and media assistance

Sign in, choose a community and select conversation, translation, grammar, writing, story practice or vocabulary. Read and select the processing consent before submitting. AI suggestions can be wrong. Generated stories are practice fiction, not verified cultural records. Vocabulary practice requires at least four distinct published dictionary translations and uses server grading.

Speech controls depend on your browser and installed voices. A missing community voice is reported instead of substituting a different language. Browser dictation may use your browser provider's service. Personal recordings stop after one minute. They remain on your device unless you separately choose AI transcription; you can preview and download them. There is no calibrated pronunciation score.

AI media tools use DeepSeek to read text from images and describe images from eligible published sources. Choose a source, give processing consent, and create a draft. Download the result and use the normal editorial process to add it to the repository. Audio transcription and AI illustration generation are currently unavailable. You can still record and play audio, upload illustrations, enter transcripts manually, and use browser dictation where supported.

## Research and settings

Open **Help & training** in the navigation menu for your account's manual. Public accounts see the public library guide; assigned staff, teaching and research accounts see their own guides. Search the topics or download the current guide. An account with several assigned duties can switch between those guides.

Public research publications, reports and datasets can be explored without privileged access. Assigned researchers and teachers see additional material only where its consent and restrictions permit. Private exports require research or curation permissions and must stay in approved storage.

Open the account menu for Settings & profile, saved conversations and sign out. The menu closes using its trigger, outside click or Escape. Interface language is separate from your assigned roles. Missing reviewed translations fall back to English; report missing wording to an editor through the project's support process.

Offline saved content and compatible learning progress are available through the existing offline library. AI, new downloads, research exports, maps and multiplayer need a connection. Synchronize queued progress after reconnecting.

## Audio, video and AI troubleshooting

To send a chat message, tick **Allow AI processing for this chat**, enter your question, then choose **Send**. The chatbot can switch to its fallback text model when the primary model reaches its quota. If a request fails, your question returns to the input so you can retry. An administrator must check the AI service configuration or quota if both models are unavailable.

Use the play controls to listen or watch. Starting another player pauses the previous one. If playback fails, choose **Try again** or download the original file. Practice voice recordings stay on your device unless you explicitly request AI transcription; device voices and dictation languages depend on the browser. If a microphone is unavailable, type your practice or upload a recording instead.

AI suggestions remain drafts. If an AI tool reports that its provider quota is reached, try later and notify an administrator. Image-generation quota is separate from text generation. Choosing a different page or repeatedly clicking Create AI draft does not restore exhausted provider quota.
