LuoLinguaAI Phase Two — Project Summary
WHAT PHASE TWO WILL DELIVER
An Android app and an iPhone app connected to the current LuoLinguaAI system.
Lessons and approved content for the dictionary, proverbs, riddles, selected stories, songs and cultural items.
Search, quizzes, saved words and simple learning progress.
Downloaded lessons that can be read without internet. Progress made offline should upload when the phone
reconnects.
AI chat that uses approved project information and shows which sources it used.
Phone notifications, email account messages, and optional SMS phone verification.
A Dholuo speech-to-text trial that the Luo developer (me) can check personally.
Real service checks: send SMS login codes and ordinary test texts, receive real account emails, and receive push
notifications on Android and iPhone. Keep a record of which tests passed and what paid usage they consumed.
A tested plan for moving the website, database and media to the JOOUST server.
Testing on Android and iPhone, store submissions, backup instructions and handover notes.

TIMELINE
Estimated development time: ~15 weeks (7 hrs/day, 5 days/week = ~525 working hours). Phase One took ~10 days (~70 hours) for
the web version.
Weeks Main work
1–2 Confirm features, approved content, store accounts, SMS needs; prepare JOOUST server access.
3–5 Build Android and iPhone app foundations and connect to existing system.
6–8 Add learning content, search, lessons, quizzes, saved words and progress.
9–10 Add downloads, offline reading and progress syncing when internet returns.
11 Add email, optional SMS codes, phone notifications, AI chat and speech-to-text trial.
12 Connect tested system to JOOUST server, move database/media, check backups, prepare store
submissions.
13–14 Test on real phones and with users, fix problems, complete store submissions.
15 Allow time for final fixes, interruptions, handover and store follow-up.
WHY PHASE TWO NEEDS ABOUT 15 WEEKS
Two phone systems: Android and iPhone use different tools and store rules. Each needs separate setup and
checks.
Offline use: Lessons must download, stay available offline, and sync later without losing progress.
Service integration: Email, SMS, push notifications, AI chat, speech-to-text and website must work together with
failure checks.
Server migration: Configure system on JOOUST server, move database/media, confirm access rules and test
backups.
Extensive testing: Real phone checks, poor internet simulations, user task testing, and bug fixing.
External factors: Network/power interruptions, account verifications, sender-name approvals, and store review
timelines.
ASSUMPTIONS AND ARRANGEMENTS
Accounts & Hosting: JOOUST provides Google Play account, Apple Developer account/Mac access, working
server/domain, and backups.
Cloud & Comms: Neon, Cloudinary, Vercel, Render used for dev. JOOUST owns Africa’s Talking SMS account,
adds credits, and provides email service access.
AI & Checks: Developer uses personal account for Gemini/STT tests initially; production keys provided by
JOOUST. Developer handles Luo language checks. No payment collection required.
