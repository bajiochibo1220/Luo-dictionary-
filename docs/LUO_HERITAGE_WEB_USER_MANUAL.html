Web Application User Manual

Edition 2.0 - 30 October 2026

Purpose: help people use the Luo Heritage website, from their first login to their assigned daily tasks.

THIS MANUAL IS FOR THE WEB APPLICATION ONLY.

Packaged Android and iOS clients share the main application interface. Android and iOS distribution, store release and physical-device acceptance remain separate deployment tasks. This manual explains the browser interface and browser installation; it does not certify native release readiness.

You can use the website in a browser on a computer, tablet or phone. A website shortcut on a phone is still the web application. It is separate from a packaged Android or iOS application.

This guide explains the current web interface reviewed in the project. Your administrator decides which version is available on your website. Some pages depend on your account permissions, approved content and connected services. If a described option is missing, use the help steps in Section 16. A feature marked Coming soon is not ready to use.

Website address: use the address provided by your JOOUST administrator.

Who should use this manual? Community members, students, teachers, contributors, cultural reviewers, editors, publishers and administrators.

How to read it: start with the quick guide. Read the staff sections only if you have a staff account. In this manual, click also means tap on a phone.

1. Start here: a quick guide

If you want to read or learn

  1. Open the website address given by your administrator.
  2. If the login screen appears, enter your email and password. Click Log in.
  3. On the dashboard, choose a subject from the left menu, such as Dictionary or Proverbs.
  4. Choose All to see all available items. Use Images, Audio, Video or Transcripts to narrow the list.
  5. Use more for the compact description or Explore this item for full details. Open an available audio, video or document using its controls.

What you should see: content that has been approved for public access. An empty subject means no matching public content is available there yet.

If you want to share something

  1. Open the subject you want to add to.
  2. Click Contribute, or Add the first… if that subject is empty.
  3. Give the item a title and complete the required fields.
  4. Check the source permission and access settings.
  5. Click Save private draft to continue later, or Submit for reviewer when ready.
  6. Check My Submissions for its status and reviewer feedback.

What you should see: your saved draft or submitted item. Submitting an item does not immediately make it public.

If you are a staff member

  1. Log in with your assigned staff account.
  2. Open the round account button at the top right.
  3. Choose Admin dashboard or Super Admin dashboard, if available.
  4. Open the queue or page for your assigned task.
  5. Read Sections 10–14 before changing records, accounts or system settings.

2. Open the website and create an account

What you need

  • A computer, tablet or phone with a browser, such as Chrome, Edge or Safari.
  • An internet connection.
  • The correct website address.
  • An email address you can access if you are creating an account.

You do not need Android Studio, a Play Store account or an App Store account to use the website.

Open the website

  1. Open your browser.
  2. Enter the website address in the address bar.
  3. Wait for the page to appear.
  4. If you are new, the website normally opens the login page. If you are already logged in, it may open your dashboard.

Do not use an address copied from an unknown message. Ask your administrator if you are unsure which website is correct.

Create an account with email and password

  1. On the login page, click Create new account.
  2. Enter your full name and email address.
  3. Enter your date of birth using the day, month and year controls.
  4. Choose a password with at least eight characters. Use Show or Hide to check it privately.
  5. Choose your language from Select your language.
  6. Tick the options that describe you: Student, Researcher, Contributor, Teacher or Community member. You may choose more than one.
  7. Read the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Tick the acceptance box only if you agree.
  8. Click the account-creation button and wait for the result.

What happens next: the website normally signs you in after creating the account. If it says the account was created but login failed, return to Sign in and use the same email and password. Do not create another account for the same person.

If you see Email already registered, use the login page. If the website says your account needs approval, ask an administrator to check its status.

Selecting Teacher or Researcher describes your interests. It does not give you staff permissions or access to protected research material.

Create an account with Google

Use this option if your administrator has enabled Google sign-up on your website.

  1. Open Create new account.
  2. Read and accept the displayed terms.
  3. Click Google and choose your own Google account.
  4. Follow Google's instructions.
  5. If Finish creating your account appears, complete your name, date of birth, language and interests. Accept the terms and click Finish creating account.

If Google cannot connect, try email/password registration or contact support. Apple, Phone and Microsoft buttons may display a coming-soon message; they are not completed sign-up options in the reviewed version.

3. Log in and sign out

Log in with email and password

  1. Open the website.
  2. Enter the email used for your account.
  3. Enter your password.
  4. Click Log in once and wait.

What you should see: your dashboard. Staff may be taken to an administrative dashboard because of their assigned role. This is expected.

If the email or password is incorrect, check spelling and whether Caps Lock is on. If your account is suspended or unavailable, contact an administrator.

Log in with Google

  1. Click Google on the login page.
  2. Choose the Google account linked to your Luo Heritage account.
  3. Complete any Google sign-in steps.

If the website says it cannot find that Google account, use Create an account to continue if you are new. If you already have an account, check that you chose the correct Google email or use your email and password.

If you forget your password

  1. Choose Forgotten password? on Log in.
  2. Enter your account email and request the code.
  3. When the code arrives, enter it with your new password and confirmation.
  4. Submit once, wait for confirmation, then return to sign in.

The reset screen contains only reset steps. Registration, login choices and account deletion do not appear during the request/confirmation steps. Email verification and deletion are separate actions in account settings. Code delivery requires a configured email service; if delivery is unavailable, contact the administrator and do not assume a password was changed. Never share a recovery code.

If the website asks you to log in again

Your login lasts for a limited time. If it expires, log in again with the same account. A new login does not mean you need a new account. Confirm that unfinished form text was saved before leaving a page.

Sign out

  1. Open the round account button at the top right.
  2. Click Sign out.
  3. Check that the login page appears.

Sign out after using a shared computer. Closing a browser window is not a substitute for signing out.

4. Find your way around

Sidebar and Back

The public sidebar begins with Install app, followed by Explore, Discover, Collections, Browse the library and Saved words and items. Additional tools depend on your account and permissions.

On a computer, the sidebar toggle collapses or expands the navigation. On a phone, it opens a drawer. Scroll inside the drawer to reach more entries; use X or choose a destination to close it. The header Back control returns to the previous screen. With no previous screen, it opens the public library.

Explore and close controls

Choose Explore beside the pinned feed search. A dialog lists Discover, available collections and Follow your curiosity links. Scroll inside the dialog to reach its last option, even on a small phone or landscape display. Back, X or Escape closes it. Selecting a collection closes the list and applies that selection.

Explore this item opens an item's complete details. Back and X stay at the top while you scroll; either closes the details and returns to the feed. Closing a panel does not save, delete or submit a contribution.

Language and account menu

Choose the community language during registration. Public sidebars and mastheads do not repeat that selector. Approved Dholuo interface labels show smaller, read-only English beneath them. Untranslated labels remain English. Content may have paired original/English versions where an editor supplied them; absent translations are not invented.

Open the round account button for Settings & profile, saved conversations, the assigned staff dashboard and Sign out. Use its Close control, press the trigger again, click outside or press Escape to dismiss it. Administrative editing controls stay in English.

5. Read, search and view cultural content

Search and filter

  1. Open Discover or a collection.
  2. Enter your words in the pinned search field and submit.
  3. Use All, Images, Audio, Video or Transcripts in the small fixed black bottom bar.
  4. Scroll the feed. Further published records load automatically when available; a retry or manual loading control helps recover from an interrupted request.

The dock remains visible while cards occupy the space above it. No matching audio or video means that type has no published item in the selected results. Text-only entries remain valid cultural records. The feed ends when the available collection ends; it does not invent additional content.

Read a card and open details

A card contains its title, category, publication/review status and a short description. On phones the description starts as one compact line; wide screens allow more room. Underlined more expands the text within the card; less returns to the compact preview. Explore this item opens the complete description, usage, meaning or place information where supplied, media, available English content, transcripts and related items.

A Review in progress badge identifies an uploaded version authorized for public display while editing continues. It is different from a completed reviewed publication. The reviewed version later replaces it at the same public link.

View media and transcripts

Images and videos fit fully inside their frame; unused space is black. Use attachment arrows to move between files. Play and pause recordings using their visible controls. Offscreen feed playback pauses so recordings do not compete. If a file fails, use the displayed retry or permitted download and report the item link if the problem continues.

Open full transcripts in the item details. The original text remains available; choose an existing English version when offered. A missing translation is reported honestly. Do not treat an automatic translation as an approved transcript.

Save, download and contribute

Sign in to save an item to your account. Download controls are available only for eligible content and files. Download complete item (ZIP) includes available descriptions, translations, transcripts and original attachments; extract it and open index.html for the offline record. Public access is not unrestricted reuse: retain attribution, source permission and any displayed rights conditions.

Use Contribute directly above the collection feed to open its form. Sign in if requested; the selected collection is retained. For drafting, review and publication, follow Sections 8?12.

6. Use Chat and saved conversations

Ask CULTURAL HERITAGE

  1. Open the floating CULTURAL HERITAGE shortcut or its navigation entry.
  2. Ask about Luo culture, history, traditions, songs, artifacts, places or Dholuo language.
  3. Read and select Allow AI processing for this chat before sending.
  4. Send once and wait. If a request fails, read the error and retry after correcting it.

The assistant focuses on Luo heritage. It can use general model knowledge as well as retrieved library sources; every answer is not necessarily backed by a repository record. Check sources and community knowledge holders for important cultural claims. Do not enter secrets, private recordings or sensitive personal information.

Translation and sources

Ask in English or Dholuo. See translation in Dholuo or See translation in English expands the answer in the other language. Collapse it when finished. Chat settings explains AI processing and the fallback language for ambiguous questions. Expand Sources and Related media when present. An AI translation can be inaccurate and needs fluent-speaker checking before publication.

Conversations and closing

Your chats opens saved conversations after sign-in. Choose New chat for a separate topic. Saved chat actions may include rename, pin, archive, restore and deletion; read confirmations before deleting. Load earlier messages retrieves older turns when offered. Guest interactions should not be relied on as saved account history.

Use X to close the assistant. Escape closes an open chat menu or history panel first, then closes the assistant. Back returns to the preceding screen. Your account menu also opens Saved conversations; use the same account that created them.

If the provider is unavailable or its quota is exhausted, wait or report the message. A successful interface load does not guarantee provider availability.

7. Change your profile options

  1. Open the round account menu and choose Settings & profile.
  2. Update your name and supported profile interests.
  3. Save once and wait for confirmation.

Community member, Student, Researcher, Contributor and Teacher interests describe your use; they do not grant administrative, editorial or publication permissions. Staff roles require an authorized assignment.

Account security contains separate Reset password and Verify email links. Request account deletion is separate and requires its own email confirmation. Email delivery must be configured for those code-based actions. Governed cultural contributions and required audit/consent evidence may be retained with restricted access; deletion is not an immediate erasure of every repository record, backup or downloaded third-party copy.

8. Add a contribution

You must be logged in to submit your own content. You do not need an administrator role to make a public-user contribution.

Open the correct form

  1. Open the subject you want to contribute to.
  2. Click Contribute or Add the first….
  3. Alternatively, open the contribution page and choose the subject under What would you like to share?
  4. Check the language and subject shown at the top of the form.

If the language is wrong, ask an administrator before submitting. The contribution language can follow your account assignment or the website's default language; the registration language and assigned administrative target determine the relevant community; verify the form before submitting.

If you see This module has no fields yet, that form is not ready. Contact an administrator.

Complete the form

  1. Enter a clear title.
  2. Complete the information you know. Each subject can have different fields, such as a word and meaning, a proverb and explanation, or a story description.
  3. Complete the fields marked with \* before submitting for review.
  4. Add an image, video, audio file or transcript/document using the matching file control, if needed.
  5. Complete Source and access carefully.
  6. Choose a saving option and wait for the result.

Do not invent a source, translation or explanation just to fill a box. Explain uncertainty to the reviewer.

Choose source permission and credit

Field or choiceSimple meaning
Source permissionWhether the speaker, knowledge holder or other relevant source has allowed this use.
Not sure yet / ask a reviewerPermission still needs checking. It does not mean permission has been given.

Anonymous credit is a preference for how the source is named. It is not a promise that authorised staff will have no source information.

Choose who may use the material

The form uses Consent scope and Restriction level. These are two separate controls.

If you choose embargoed, complete Embargo ends. That date does not automatically publish an item. If you are unsure about any setting, keep a draft and ask a reviewer.

Public visibility needs more than selecting public. The approved content, its source permission and its attached files must meet the release rules. Never change a restriction just to make an upload appear on the public page.

The form may also offer location, source reference and collection details. Use agreed details from your reviewer; do not make up a collection/session number.

Save now or submit for review

  • Save private draft: keep your work for later. This is not public publication.
  • Submit for reviewer: send the completed item to Review & edit.

The contribution form is not a continuous autosave service. Click a save button and wait for confirmation before closing the page.

What you should see: the website normally opens My Submissions with your saved item and status.

Attach files and handle an upload failure

Use the matching image, video or audio control. The transcript/document control accepts the displayed supported types, including text, PDF and Word documents. Accepted types and file limits are enforced by the website.

Wait until the save and upload finish. If an upload fails, read the message and check My Submissions before trying again. A record may already have been saved. Reopen that item rather than creating a duplicate contribution. Keep the original file until you have confirmed the saved attachment opens.

Record an oral-history narration in the browser

Use this only where the Oral Histories form offers it.

  1. Confirm the speaker's permission and select Permission confirmed.
  2. Click Start recording.
  3. Allow microphone access if your browser asks.
  4. Speak, then click Stop recording.
  5. Check that the form says the audio is attached.
  6. Save a private draft or submit the item for review.

Stopping the recording does not save the contribution by itself. If recording is unsupported or permission is refused, use an existing permitted audio file instead. Staff must still check access before release.

9. Check your submissions and make corrections

Find your work

After saving a contribution, check My Submissions. To reopen the page later, use the page address /my-submissions on the same website if there is no visible menu link. Section 18 explains how.

You should see your own items, their dates, status labels and available reviewer feedback.

Understand a status label

Correct an item when editing is available

  1. Read Reviewer feedback.
  2. Click Edit / complete translations on the item.
  3. Correct the details the reviewer identified.
  4. Complete any available translation fields.
  5. Save the changes or resubmit using the action the form offers.
  6. Return to My Submissions and check the new status.

Editing is available only at allowed stages. If there is no edit link, the item may be locked for review or publication. Ask the reviewer rather than submitting a duplicate to bypass that stage.

If an item is not visible publicly, check its status and permission settings with the reviewer. Submitting, approving internally and publishing publicly are different steps.

11. Review, edit and publish content

Prepare and review

  1. The contributor saves a draft or submits the completed source material.
  2. An assigned editor opens Review & edit and checks cultural accuracy, source consent, attribution, restrictions, files and both language versions.
  3. Correct the source-language and English text together, or return work with a clear explanation.
  4. Send suitable reviewed content to Publishing queue.
  5. An authorized publisher checks eligibility and publishes the reviewed version.

Review permissions and workflow stages are enforced by the server. Use available rejection/history controls when needed; do not create duplicate records to bypass a locked stage. Content editing is not a licence to publish private, sacred or unauthorized knowledge.

Publish an uploaded version while editing continues

Authorized staff may use Publish before review on eligible uploads, including their own uploads where permitted. Publish selected before review handles a selection when the account is authorized for every item. Read the selected titles and confirm Publish now.

This publishes a separate snapshot of the upload and its attachments while the source remains in review. It overrides saved access restrictions and embargoes for that public release, and records the staff decision and previous settings. It does not manufacture rights-holder permission: check actual source authority before using it. On the public feed it is marked Review in progress.

Continue editing privately. Final publication from Publishing queue replaces the earlier snapshot at the same public link. Rejection or removal of the public/source permission can withdraw the early version. Existing published packages have their own withdrawal rules and cannot be removed by ordinary queue deletion.

Check the public result

After publication, open the public collection on a phone and desktop. Confirm title, category, status, compact description, complete item details, English wording, visible image/audio/video attachments, transcripts and permitted downloads. Check linked related records and separately approved public map coordinates. Private collection GPS must not become public merely because a file is attached.

Edit interface wording

Use Edit system text for approved Dholuo interface labels beside their English meaning. Save a small batch and preview public navigation, login, search, recovery messages and Help & training. Blank translations fall back to English. Do not enter machine-generated cultural wording without fluent review.

History, corrections and removal

Use Review history, Published history and Audit Logs to follow responsible actions. Delete or withdraw only using the controls and authority available for the item's stage. Read confirmation for single or batch actions. Retained audit history is separate from what public users can browse. Report consent, privacy or culturally sensitive publication errors promptly through the institution's support route.

12. Manage media, transcripts and translations

These pages are for staff with the relevant permissions. A page name in a menu does not give permission to all of its files.

Upload several files as drafts

Where Bulk media upload appears:

  1. Check the selected language and subject.
  2. Click Select files and choose up to twenty supported files.
  3. Remove an unwanted queued file with its removal control before starting.
  4. Click Upload … items as drafts.
  5. Wait while the files upload one at a time.
  6. Open Content and complete the draft information, permissions and review steps.

If part of the batch fails, use Retry batch while that batch remains on the page. Completed uploads in the same batch are retained. This does not promise that an unfinished batch survives closing the browser. Check the Content list before starting a new batch.

Use the Media page

  1. Open Media.
  2. Use the offered type filters and open a file's details.
  3. Preview the image, recording or document where allowed.
  4. Use Open in new tab when available.

If an unattached file offers Attach to content, choose the correct published entry and confirm the attachment. Check the file's source and access conditions before linking it. Attaching a private file to a public item does not itself grant public permission.

Delete from Cloudinary is a permanent file-removal action. Use it only when you are authorised and have checked the correct file and its use in other records. It is not a way to close a preview or remove an item from a temporary list.

Read research transcripts

  1. Open Transcripts.
  2. Choose a collection session.
  3. Click a segment heading to expand its text.
  4. Use Expand all segments or Collapse all segments when helpful.

A transcript is written text from collected material. The presence of a transcript does not prove there is an audio recording available to play. Public item details offer the available transcript and text-download controls. Research transcript tools remain permission-controlled; use only the segment, translation and download controls actually offered for your account.

Research-only, restricted and sacred material is not the public library. Being a registered user, choosing Researcher in your profile, or seeing a session title does not by itself grant access. Request the required permission from the custodian.

Change translations

Use Translations to edit the module titles and field labels offered for your permitted language. Type the approved wording, click Save, and wait for confirmation.

To translate an individual contribution, use its English version or available translation form instead. Click Save translation when finished.

Changing a label is different from translating the content of every record. Do not overwrite the original-language record with an English translation.

Read Analytics, AI Monitoring and Audit Logs

PageWhat to do
AnalyticsChoose an offered time period and language filter. Read the counts and charts in that context.
AI MonitoringUse Chatbot, Embeddings and Flagged tabs to inspect the information allowed to your role.

In AI Monitoring, authorised reviewers can use Flag to mark a response for attention. It is a review marker, not proof that the answer has been corrected. Embeddings refers to the AI search index; technical rebuilding actions should be handled with the technical team.

Counts, logs and green health indicators are useful checks. They do not by themselves prove that every feature, backup restore or AI answer has passed a complete test.

13. Manage users and administrators

Manage accounts through Users

  1. Open Users if your role permits it.
  2. Open the correct person and check the email before changing anything.
  3. Edit only the fields that your account is allowed to change.
  4. Click Save Profile and wait for confirmation.

The list may be limited to your assigned languages. If account-status controls are available, Suspend selected stops those accounts from using their allowed access; Activate selected restores active status subject to their assigned roles.

Do not select multiple people unless you intend the same status change for all of them. Some senior accounts are protected from these actions.

Create a staff account through Admins

Use this process when your account is authorised to appoint staff.

  1. Open Super Admin dashboard, then Admins.
  2. Click Add Admin.
  3. Enter the required email, name and password.
  4. Choose the approved staff role and the language or languages it should cover.
  5. Confirm the form's create action.
  6. Ask the new staff member to log in and check that the correct workspace is available.

Use the approved staff-account process rather than assuming that an ordinary public profile can be converted by adding an interest or a role. If an email already belongs to a public account, ask the Master administrator how that person should receive staff access.

Super Admin appointments and other protected actions depend on the appointing person's authority. A Super Admin is not automatically allowed to create another Super Admin.

Change or remove a staff role

Where role controls are offered:

  1. Open the correct staff account.
  2. Check the language and existing roles.
  3. Choose the permitted role and language, then click Add.
  4. To remove a role, use its removal control and read the confirmation.

Only permitted senior accounts can do this. A successful role change affects access; the person may need to log in again to see their current workspace.

On Admins, Deactivate, Activate and Revoke have different purposes. Deactivation changes account availability; revocation removes administrative access. Read the confirmation before choosing either.

Delete User permanently removes the account through the allowed management process. It is not the same as suspension. Ordinary users request deletion separately in Settings & profile and confirm by email. Governed contributions and required consent/audit evidence can be retained with restricted access.

14. Manage languages, settings and backups

Add or activate a language

For accounts permitted to use Languages:

  1. Open Languages, then Add Language.
  2. Enter the language code, name and native name.
  3. If assigning a Language Admin in the wizard, enter that person's existing account email.
  4. Check the module titles offered by the wizard.
  5. Review the details and finish the creation step.
  6. Prepare the language's labels, access arrangements and content.
  7. Open its details and choose Activate when it is ready.

The wizard creates a new language inactive. Activation makes it available under the website's rules; it does not fill it with approved cultural content. Deactivate withdraws its active availability without being the same as deleting it. The page can prevent deletion when records exist.

Use System settings

Open System only if your account is authorised to manage it. The reviewed tabs include General, API Keys, Email, Storage, Maintenance and Terms & Privacy. Some controls are reserved for the Master Super Admin.

TabWhat it is for
GeneralThe general website settings offered on the page.
API KeysConnection settings for services such as AI. Ask the technical team to help with these.
EmailSender and delivery-provider settings. Save first, then use Send test email to me if authorised.
StorageThe website's file-storage connection.
MaintenanceTemporarily limit access while work is being carried out and show a maintenance message.
Terms & PrivacyThe text displayed on the website's public Terms and Privacy pages.

For a setting change, check the current value, enter the approved replacement, click that tab's Save button and wait for confirmation. Do not change a service key merely because an unrelated page is loading slowly. Keep service keys out of screenshots and shared documents.

Website notifications are already separate from email delivery. Saving email settings or sending one test email does not complete every account-email, SMS or notification feature.

Turning maintenance on can limit ordinary users' access. Tell the relevant users when planned maintenance affects them. Check public access after turning it off.

Save and publish legal documents makes the entered Terms and Privacy text public. Use the approved text and verified operator details. This is not a private draft-save action.

Create and download a database backup

The reviewed Backup page is reserved for the Master Super Admin.

  1. Open Backup.
  2. Click Backup Now.
  3. Wait for Backup created.
  4. Check the new filename, date and size in the list.
  5. Click Download and save the file in the approved secure location.

A backup can contain private account and research information. Share it only through the approved management process.

A database backup stores database information. It is not a guarantee that all original images, audio and video files have also been copied. Ask the technical team to maintain and check file backups separately.

The web page offers backup creation, download and deletion. It does not provide a Restore button. Restoration and a full recovery test need the technical team. Do not delete the only useful recovery copy.

15. Use a website shortcut and web updates

Install the browser application

  1. Open Install app, the first sidebar action.
  2. If your browser offers native installation, confirm its prompt.
  3. Open the installed icon after installation completes.

Some browsers only offer installation when their eligibility requirements are met. Safari on iPhone uses Share, then Add to Home Screen. If installation is unavailable, follow the displayed platform instructions or continue in a browser tab. A browser installation is separate from the packaged Android/iOS release.

Offline use and shared devices

An installed app still needs online services for authentication, AI, new media, submissions and other connected actions. Previously saved offline resources and compatible learning progress are available through the offline library; some progress synchronizes after reconnecting. Do not assume every page is cached or every form is queued.

Sign out on shared devices. Downloaded files and offline copies may remain until removed. Save or synchronize progress before clearing site/device storage, as unsynchronized local work can be lost.

Updates

When Update available appears, save unfinished work before choosing Update now. Choose Later to finish first. App updates & installation also offers a manual update check. If a check cannot connect, retry when online. A renamed icon or updated shortcut does not change your account's submitted records.

  • The exact error message, if any.
  • The date and approximate time.
